---
name: Customer menu mode
description: Public customer menu behavior and the employee POS exception.
---

Keep the public customer experience as a browse-only electronic menu. Customer registration remains available, but customer cart, checkout, kiosk, table-order, and delivery-order flows stay disabled. Employee/POS order entry must remain available.

**Why:** the user asked for a menu rather than a customer ordering website, while explicitly preserving staff/POS ordering.

**How to apply:** Keep menu browsing and customer registration routes accessible; do not disable employee sessions or cashier order creation when changing public ordering behavior.
