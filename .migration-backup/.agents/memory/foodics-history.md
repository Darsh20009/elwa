---
name: Foodics historical sales
description: Rules for incorporating the available Foodics monthly sales summary into Elwa.
---

Treat Foodics historical sales as monthly aggregate-only inputs. Include their totals in Elwa's all-time and relevant branch financial summaries, but do not fabricate individual order records or imply daily/order-level history.

**Why:** The source export contains monthly totals only, and Elwa should carry these figures without presenting them as a separate Foodics report or inventing missing transaction details.

**How to apply:** Keep the monthly source distinct from transaction records, add it only to aggregate dashboard summaries, and label it as monthly summary data.
